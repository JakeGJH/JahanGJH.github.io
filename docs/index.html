About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Industrial and Systems Engineering and a graduate research assistant at the University of Houston, working under the supervision of Prof. Kailai Wang. My research focuses on transportation cybersecurity and public transit recovery.

I use optimization, spatial analysis, and empirical methods to study recovery and planning decisions, with attention to passenger access and service continuity.

Research Interests

  • Transportation Cybersecurity and Resilience: transit disruptions, passenger needs, and recovery decisions.
  • Travel Behavior and Policy: travel preferences and stated responses to transit and parking policies.
  • Accessibility, Equity, and Electrification: transit access and the spatial coverage of public EV charging.
  • Geospatial Analysis: spatial and temporal associations between extreme weather and recorded crime.
